## Two tooling decisions the team should know about

**1. Leading จง removed from 21 reworded prompts.** The reviewer prefixed imperative prompts with จง ("จงเขียน…", "จงเรียงลำดับ…"). Batch 1's 163 items — already through two native readers — use the bare imperative ("เขียน 2-4 ประโยค…", "จัดลำดับ…"), and the translation brief asks for a formal-conversational rather than school-exam register. To keep the two batches consistent, the reviewer's rewording was kept in full and only the leading จง dropped. If the team prefers จง, it is a one-line change to apply across all 252 items; say so and both batches will be aligned the other way.

**2. Keyword hygiene after the reviewer's expansion.** `scoreTextAnswer` does substring matching, so very short stems score almost any answer. Five stems were replaced with longer ones carrying the same meaning: คน → โดยคน / มนุษย์ (three criteria), รอ → รอก่อน / รอให้, PR → ประชาสัมพันธ์ / ฝ่ายสื่อสาร (lower-cased "pr" is a substring of "prompt"). After that, every Thai exemplar answer was re-scored through the app's own function; three criteria that the exemplar no longer hit got one or two extra stems (ตนเอง, ไม่ควรถือเป็นหลักฐาน, ปรับแนวทาง) so all 18 free-text items score 98 on their exemplar. No reviewer keyword was removed other than those five.
